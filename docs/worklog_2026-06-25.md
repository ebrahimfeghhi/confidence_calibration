## KEY TAKEAWAYS

- **Anchor accumulation is the load-bearing mechanism** — it turns a "push" into "search by
  elimination" (cross off B1→B2→decoys until only G remains; that's why G appears at runs 4–9,
  never run 1). One-shot repulsion just falls into B2.
- Of the "hard" hyperparameters: **M is solved** (stop at the repel plateau), **R is
  self-calibrating** (loop-until-dry via accumulation), **nudge is robust**, leaving **K** as the
  one genuinely open knob.
- Honest gaps: **n=1 pair** (only 7txw — generality untested), **label-free G recognition** on a
  novel pair (ipTM has the B2 trap), and **beating the lottery on raw FLOPs is not yet shown**
  (our win is directedness/reliability + reusing one ESMC rep).

self-repulsion loss + anchor accumulation and only swapped the lever. Implemented as
`repel_proven.py --lever affine` (s_lm path byte-identical to the validated recipe).
**Running overnight: `affine_lr02` (lr 0.02) and `affine_lr10` (lr 0.1), M=12, K=3, 5 trials × 10
runs.** Hypothesis open — a global affine may be too constrained to produce a localized
reorientation; if it reaches G consistently, it's a lower-dim, geometry-preserving lever worth
preferring.
