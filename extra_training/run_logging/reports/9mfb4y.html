The four terms are drawn beneath it for composition; they happen to sum to the objective whenever alpha_confidence and alpha_pae are both 1 (here 1.0 and 1.0), but the curve does not depend on that holding. One point is the mean over the 85 poses rank 0 accumulated for that step — not one pose, and not all 255: the trajectory is written by rank 0 and the trainer all-reduces the gradient but not the loss scalar, so the other 2 ranks' poses are not in this curve.
